Metric definition:
- ROIs are frozen Aligned-Ply coordinate boxes selected from the accepted G1.1 views;
  they are not frontend-centered/display-coordinate boxes.
- Dominant plane: fixed-seed RANSAC on at most 50,000 selected points, then SVD refit.
  `robust_plane.rms_distance` is the RMS of all selected inliers within 0.03 arbitrary units.
- Normal dispersion: P50/P90 angular deviation of grid-local SVD normals from the refit
  dominant normal. Thickness is the selected ROI's normal-distance P95-P05.
- Repeated layers: count non-maximum-suppressed normal-distance histogram peaks, with
  0.02 bin width and at least 0.05 separation. Coverage is occupied cells in a 20x20
  robust plane-coordinate grid. These are arbitrary-scale diagnostic measures, not metres.

  take deterministic uncovered candidates and the selection record explicitly reports
