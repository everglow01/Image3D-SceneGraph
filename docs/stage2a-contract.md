# Stage 2A: static 3DGS benchmark and dataset contract

This document freezes the inputs and isolation rules used by Stage 2 algorithm development. Generated images, camera outputs, contracts, checkpoints, and render evaluations remain under ignored `data/` and `outputs/` paths.

`terrains` remains the immediately executable public geometry-development scene. It is not used with ETH3D reference poses or scans during 3DGS training: the contract consumes only RGB and the project-produced COLMAP camera file. Its rendering results must not be mixed with Stage 1 scan-based geometry metrics.

Mip-NeRF 360 `room` is the primary public indoor product proxy because its camera path and scene layout are closer to indoor free-viewpoint viewing than the object-centric `bonsai` scene. It still covers one bounded room, has arbitrary scale, and does not establish whole-home coverage, room connectivity, metric accuracy, or a production VR-tour guarantee. `bonsai` remains the public fine-detail and literature-reference scene rather than being discarded.

Both Mip-NeRF 360 scenes are present locally from the official `360_v2.zip`; its project page provides no dataset-specific license or redistribution statement. The data remains ignored and local, and no redistribution or unrestricted-use claim is made.

## Deterministic view protocol

- Minimum: 12 successfully registered perspective images.
- Split seed: `20260729`.
- Target ratio: 80/10/10; validation and test each contain at least two views. Rounding uses `max(2, round(N * 0.1))` for each held-out split and assigns the remainder to train.
- Camera-spatial selection uses deterministic farthest-point traversal over camera centers. The first point is farthest from the centroid; hash-based tie-breaking depends on seed and image ID. Alternating held-out points go to test and validation.
- Upload order and filename adjacency do not define the split.
- Train views may optimize model parameters. Validation may monitor, select checkpoints, and compare at most eight candidates for one research question. Test remains unread until the candidate and effective configuration are frozen; test never controls gradients, early stopping, checkpoint selection, or hyperparameters.
- Any change to source images, camera source, selected views, split seed, poses, intrinsics, or normalization changes `dataset_hash` and creates a new dataset version.

## RTX 4060 development envelope

R2.1 freezes limits, not the final training profile (that belongs to R2.14):

- hardware: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4060 Laptop GPU, 8,188 MiB reported memory, compute capability 8.9;
- execution: one GPU-heavy process at a time;
- input: JPG/PNG, 12–100 registered views, ≤30 MiB per file and ≤1 GiB per job;
- benchmark selection: 32 views for private and current public development scenes;
- initial training render ceiling: longest edge 1,280 px; lower explicit profiles may be used, but silent adaptive resizing is forbidden;
- Gaussian ceiling before R2.14 evidence: 1,000,000;
- experiment budget: at most 8 validation candidates per question and 24 GPU-hours per round;
- every run records effective resolution, Gaussian count, peak allocated and reserved CUDA memory, training/render time, checkpoint/export size, OOM/failure reason, code/dependency versions, and dataset/config hashes;
- OOM is a failed attempt unless an explicitly named profile downgrade starts a new auditable attempt. Partial output is never successful.

A promotion needs held-out quality, resource use, and failure rate together. Rendering PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS must stay separate from ETH3D Accuracy/Completeness/F1 and must not be described as metric reconstruction accuracy.

## Dataset/camera contract v1

The executable implementation is `image3d_scenegraph.gaussian.dataset`.

- `images`: unique project-relative paths, SHA-256, dimensions, 3×3 intrinsics, explicit distortion state/parameters, and paired 4×4 poses.
- camera convention: OpenCV camera axes (`+x` right, `+y` down, `+z` forward); `camera_from_world` maps world points to camera coordinates, and `world_from_camera` is its checked inverse.
- world frame: `raw`; units are `arbitrary`. Viewer-only axis flips are never written into this contract.
- Raw/Aligned: an aligned dataset must be a new contract with an explicit rigid/similarity transform and transformed cameras/initialization. It must not relabel raw assets or imply metres.
- normalization: camera-center mean and maximum radius, stored as inverse 4×4 `normalized_from_world` and `world_from_normalized` transforms. Training may use normalized space; canonical export can invert it. A render camera is rigid in normalized camera units: the uniform scale in `world_from_normalized` is removed before passing the view matrix to gsplat, so its explicit `near_plane=0.01` and `far_plane=1e10` are interpreted in normalized units rather than raw COLMAP arbitrary units.
- readiness: before any Gaussian CUDA process starts, `gaussian_geometry_readiness_v1` records robust camera-center and initialization-scale distributions. One isolated camera at least 100× the median radius and 10× p99, or at least 50% of initial scales pinned to the 3NN floor, is an explicit unusable-geometry failure rather than an OOM-prone training attempt. The check reads pose/split metadata and initialization arrays, not Test RGB or Test metrics.
- splits: image IDs only, exhaustive and pairwise disjoint.
- initialization: optional project-relative asset and hash, always with its coordinate frame. Sparse/dense policy is not chosen in 2A.
- source and effective dataset hashes: camera source and every selected image are hashed; absolute local paths are not serialized.

The current adapter accepts the existing project `colmap_world` JSON and these camera models: `SIMPLE_PINHOLE`, `PINHOLE`, `SIMPLE_RADIAL`, `RADIAL`, and `OPENCV`. Nonzero distortion remains explicit as `modeled`; training must undistort or implement that model before consuming those pixels. Unsupported, missing, non-finite, duplicate, escaping-path, hash-mismatched, or fewer-than-12-view inputs fail before training.

Legacy imported splats are format/viewer fixtures only and are not dataset, checkpoint, or evaluation evidence.
